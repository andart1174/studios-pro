Frequently Asked Questions Foire Aux Questions (FAQ)

Everything you need to know about our 24+ browser-based 3D, 4D, Multi-Color 3MF, WebAR, and CNC/Laser CAD studios. Tout ce qu'il faut savoir sur nos 24+ studios 3D, 4D, 3MF Multi-Couleurs, WebAR et CAO CNC/Laser directement dans le navigateur.

Q1: Do I need to install any software to use Studios-Pro?

A1: No. Studios-Pro is a 100% browser-based platform. Whether you are painting multi-color 3MF models, sculpting 3D/4D photo reliefs, designing rings in Jewelry Maker Pro, or generating CNC G-Code, all computations run directly in Chrome, Safari, Edge, or Firefox with zero installation.

Q2: What file formats can I export from Studios-Pro?

A2: Studios-Pro supports STL, OBJ, 3MF (multi-color Bambu Studio / OrcaSlicer / Prusa MMU), GLB, GLTF, PLY, and USDZ/WebAR for 3D printing and augmented reality; SVG, DXF, and GRBL/Marlin G-Code for laser cutters and CNC routers; plus WAV audio, MP4/WebM video, and standalone interactive HTML files.

Q3: Is my generated content and 3D data private?

A3: Yes. All 3D rendering, mesh generation, photo relief sculpting, and CAD conversions execute locally on your device using WebGL and WebAssembly. Your uploaded photos, STL/3MF models, and DXF blueprints are never uploaded to our servers.

Q4: How does the Free vs. Premium system work?

A4: Every visitor can explore and design across all 24+ creative studios for free, and free accounts include 5 complimentary production exports. Upgrading to Studios-Pro Premium unlocks unlimited exports, high-resolution outputs, commercial usage rights, and full access across every studio.

Q5: What is the price of Studios-Pro Premium?

A5: Studios-Pro Premium costs $10 USD per month (billed securely via Stripe). There are no hidden fees or per-model credits — one subscription covers unlimited exports across all 24+ studios.

Q6: Can I use Studios-Pro on mobile phones, tablets, and Chromebooks?

A6: Yes. Studios-Pro is fully responsive and works on iOS, Android, iPadOS, macOS, Windows, Linux, and Chromebooks. For heavy high-polygon 3D sculpting or long video renders, a desktop or laptop with hardware GPU acceleration delivers the fastest performance.

Q7: Is Studios-Pro a free online alternative to HueForge and Bambu AMS color tools?

A7: Yes! Our Filament Painting & Layer-Swap Studio uses Linear-RGB Kubelka-Munk optical transmission distance (TD) simulation, a free smartphone Camera TD calibrator, 250+ spools (.hfp import), CMYW color lithophanes, and native Bambu Studio / OrcaSlicer auto-swap 3MF export. Pair it with our Multi-Color 3MF Painter for 1-click AI colorization on STL models.

Q8: Can I view my 3D models in Augmented Reality (AR) on my phone without an app?

A8: Yes. Upload any STL, OBJ, or GLB file in Studios-Pro AR Viewer (or click AR directly inside any studio) and scan the QR code with your phone. Your model opens at real-world scale via native Android SceneViewer and iOS QuickLook with zero app download.

Q9: Does Studios-Pro work for 3D printing, laser cutting, and CNC routers?

A9: Absolutely. Studios-Pro outputs watertight binary STL and multi-part 3MF files ready for Bambu Lab, Prusa, Creality, Anycubic, and Elegoo slicers, plus scale-accurate DXF, SVG, and GRBL/Marlin G-Code toolpaths for diode/CO2 lasers and CNC mills.

Q10: Can I design 3D watch movements, Cryptex puzzles, and papercraft in PolyMorph 3D Studio?

A10: Yes. PolyMorph 3D Studio includes procedural engines for mechanical watch movements and tourbillons, Da Vinci Cryptex puzzle boxes, Sugihara optical illusions, Pepakura-style papercraft unfolders, modular RPG dungeon tiles, flexi articulated models, and Voronoi vases with instant AR projection.

Q11: Can I sell 3D prints, coasters, or CAD designs created with Studios-Pro (Commercial License)?

A11: Free tier exports are licensed for personal, educational, and evaluation use. Active Studios-Pro Premium subscribers receive full commercial rights to sell physical 3D prints, laser-cut products, jewelry, coasters, and custom client deliverables generated with our studios. Full terms are published in our Site Rules.

Q12: How do cancellations, billing, and data privacy (GDPR / Quebec Law 25) work?

A12: All payments are processed securely by Stripe — we never store credit card numbers. You can cancel your Premium subscription at any time before renewal, and you can request full account and data deletion at any time in accordance with GDPR and Quebec Law 25 via our Contact form or Privacy Policy.
