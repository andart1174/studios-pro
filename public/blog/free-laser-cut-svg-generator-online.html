What File Format Do Laser Cutters Use? Quel format de fichier les découpeuses laser utilisent-elles ?

Most laser cutters (like the xTool D1, Sculpfun S30, or Ortur Laser Master) accept SVG vector files or DXF CAD files as input. They read the vector paths and trace them with the laser beam for cutting or engraving. SVG is preferred for simpler workflows (compatible with LightBurn, K40 Whisperer), while DXF is used for professional CNC machines and CAD workflows. G-Code files contain the precise machine movement instructions and are used for the most precise control.
