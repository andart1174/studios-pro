How Does AI Convert a 2D Image to a 3D Model? Comment l'IA convertit-elle une image 2D en modèle 3D ?

AI-powered depth estimation analyzes a 2D photograph and predicts the relative depth (distance from camera) of each pixel. Bright pixels are interpreted as closer to the viewer (high elevation) and dark pixels as farther away (low elevation). This grayscale depth map is then converted into a 3D heightmap mesh: a surface with varying Z-height values that produce a 3D relief sculpture when 3D printed. The result is a tangible physical object that visually represents the original photograph in three dimensions. L'estimation de profondeur IA analyse une photographie 2D et prédit la profondeur relative de chaque pixel. Les pixels clairs sont interprétés comme plus proches (haute élévation) et les pixels sombres comme plus éloignés. Cette carte de profondeur est convertie en maillage 3D heightmap.
