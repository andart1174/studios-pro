Site Rules, Terms of Service & Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 2026 • Applies to studios-pro.com & all 24+ browser studios

1. Privacy Policy & Personal Data (GDPR & Quebec Law 25)

Studios-Pro processes all 3D mesh generation, lithophane relief calculations, 3MF painting, CNC vectorization, and WebGL rendering locally inside your browser whenever possible. Your design source files are never permanently stored on our servers.

However, to operate accounts, subscriptions, and analytics, we collect limited personal information strictly when required:

  • Account & Authentication (Google Firebase): Your email address, display name (if using Google Sign-In), user ID, and export counter.
  • Billing (Stripe): Processed exclusively by Stripe (PCI-DSS compliant). Studios-Pro never sees or stores your credit card number.
  • Contact & Newsletter Forms (Netlify Forms & Firestore): Your name, email address, and message content when you voluntarily contact us or subscribe.
  • Temporary Mobile AR Share & Live Collab: If you explicitly click "Share to Mobile AR QR", a temporary `.glb` file is cached for 7 days via Netlify Blobs so your phone can download it. Collaborative scripting rooms transmit code only inside rooms you join while signed in.
  • Your Rights & Data Deletion: Under GDPR and Quebec Law 25, you may request access, correction, or complete deletion of your account and personal data at any time via the Contact form or by contacting our Privacy Officer (Responsable de la protection des renseignements personnels) at support@studios-pro.com.

2. Cookies & Analytics

Studios-Pro uses browser localStorage to save your language preference (EN/FR), studio drafts, and guest export count. We also use Google Analytics 4 (GA4) and Google Ads conversion tags to measure site performance and improve our tools. You can block or clear cookies at any time through your browser settings.

3. Free Plan, Pro Subscription ($10 USD/mo), Cancellation & Refunds

  • Free Tier: Every visitor receives 5 free exports (2 guest exports + 3 additional exports after creating a free account) with zero credit card required.
  • Studios-Pro Unlimited ($10 USD / month): Auto-renewing monthly subscription managed securely via Stripe, unlocking unlimited exports across all 24+ studios, HD CNC/3D reliefs, watermark-free standalone HTML exports, and commercial usage rights.
  • Cancellation & Refunds: You may cancel your subscription at any time before your next billing date with zero penalty; Pro access remains active through the end of your paid billing period. For billing issues or refund requests within 7 days of accidental renewal, contact us via the Contact form or support@studios-pro.com.

  • Your Creations: You retain 100% ownership of the original artwork, photos, CAD scripts, and 3D models you create using Studios-Pro.
  • Free Plan License: Exports produced under the Free plan are licensed for personal, educational, and non-commercial prototyping use.
  • Pro Commercial License: Active Studios-Pro Unlimited subscribers receive a full commercial license to manufacture, 3D-print, laser-cut, CNC-mill, and sell physical products or digital assets created with our studios (including STL, 3MF, OBJ, GLB, DXF, SVG, G-Code, and HTML outputs).
  • User Responsibility: You are solely responsible for ensuring that any image, logo, or 3D file you upload does not infringe third-party copyrights or trademarks.

You agree not to attempt unauthorized access, automated scraping/abuse of our endpoints, circumvention of export quotas, or submission of malicious scripts.
